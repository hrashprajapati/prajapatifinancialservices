About Harsh

Welcome to Prajapati Investment & Financial Services

This business was started by my father, Ramkishor Prajapati, 20 years ago. He began by filing income tax returns and distributing mutual funds, and over the years expanded into a full range of other financial products and services. Today, I carry that legacy forward.

I help individuals and families build wealth through disciplined financial planning and investment strategies. I am a registered financial advisor, having completed the NISM Investment Adviser Level 1 and Level 2 certifications, along with certifications in mutual fund distribution, portfolio management services, and derivatives across currency, interest rate, and equity segments.

Every recommendation starts with understanding where you are today and where you want to be, then mapping mutual funds and investment strategies that fit your timeline, risk appetite, and cash flow.
